Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 15, 2026

Trades is in beta. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it. We will update it as the product evolves.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Trades ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the website and services at findtrades.co (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide:

  • Account details: your name, email address, company name, password, and the roles your company performs (contractor and/or subcontractor).
  • Company profile: location and service area, trades, crew size, availability, website, phone, about text, photos, and self-reported license, insurance, and credential information.
  • Marketplace activity: jobs you post, bids you submit, awards, reviews, network connections, invitations, referrals, and calendar events.
  • Communications: messages you send to other companies, and feedback or support requests you send to us.

Information collected automatically:

  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in.
  • Basic technical and usage information needed to operate and secure the Service.
  • Approximate location derived from the city, state, and address details you enter (we use mapping services to improve radius matching).

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: create your account, build your profile, match jobs and companies by location and trade, and enable bids, messages, and reviews.
  • To send you transactional emails, such as password resets, sign-in links, and notifications about jobs, bids, awards, and messages.
  • To maintain safety and integrity, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms.
  • To improve and develop the Service.

4. How we share information

  • With other users: your company profile, location, trades, posted jobs, reviews, and the content of conversations and bids are shared with the users they are intended for, as part of how the marketplace works.
  • With service providers who help us operate the Service, including email delivery (Resend), hosting (Railway), and mapping (Google Maps). They may process information only to provide services to us.
  • For legal and safety reasons: to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, property, and safety of users, the public, or Trades.
  • In a business transfer: if Trades is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Cookies

We use an essential session cookie so you stay signed in. We do not use the cookie for advertising.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can ask us to delete your account as described below.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including hashed passwords and access controls. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. License, insurance, and credential information is self-reported by users and is not verified by us unless we state otherwise.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can review and update most of your information in your account profile.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, or closure of your account, by contacting us.
  • You can opt out of non-essential notification emails; we may still send essential account and security messages.

9. Third-party services and links

The Service relies on third-party providers and may link to third-party sites. Their use of your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

10. Children

The Service is intended for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and may provide additional notice.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Contact us at support@findtrades.co.
